Hidden Gems of Hanoi: A First-Timer’s Guide to Street Food

Stepping into Hanoi’s Old Quarter for the first time feels like walking straight into a living, breathing movie set. Motorbikes weave effortlessly through narrow streets, tiny plastic stools spill onto every sidewalk, and the rich aroma of simmering pho broth fills the air.

If you are planning your first trip to Vietnam’s capital, skip the overly touristy checklist and spend a slow day getting lost in its authentic corners—from sipping warm egg coffee on a hidden balcony to grabbing smoky Bun Cha from a sidewalk charcoal grill.
